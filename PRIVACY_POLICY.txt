Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 26, 2026

1. General
    This application is a web game "Memory Game". We value your privacy and do not collect your personal data on external servers.

2. What data do we process and why?
    We use the Local Storage mechanism of your browser to:
      - Save the selected difficulty level (number of pairs).
      - Maintain the "Score Table" (player name, number of moves, data).

3. Data storage
    - Locality: Your data never leaves your device.
    - Right to delete: You can delete all data at any time by clicking the "Clear history" button in the score table or by clearing your browser cache.
    - Cookies: We only use technical cookies necessary to remember your consent when using the cookie policy.

4.  Contacts
    If you have any questions about the game, please contact the developer vt231_pdm@student.ztu.edu.ua.
